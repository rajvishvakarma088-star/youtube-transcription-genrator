    GOOD TO KNOW

    Frequently asked questions.

    Is it free to use?

    Yes. Generate transcripts from available YouTube captions for free.

    Can I translate a transcript?

    Yes, when YouTube provides a caption or translation track in your chosen language.

    Can I edit or download the transcript?

    Yes. Use Edit text, Remove selected, Copy, or Download .txt in the transcript workspace.

    Does it transcribe videos without captions?

    This tool reads YouTube caption tracks. Videos without available captions cannot be transcribed here.
