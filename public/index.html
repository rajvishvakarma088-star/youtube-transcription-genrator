    Connecting to downloader…

    This feature saves files on your computer through a local companion. Get the project from GitHub, place it in ~/Downloads/youtube-transcription-genrator, then run cd ~/Downloads/youtube-transcription-genrator && ./start-downloader.sh. Click Connect below and allow this site to access the local companion when your browser asks. Each person needs the companion running on their own computer.

    YouTube downloads are currently unavailable from this cloud server. For videos you uploaded, use YouTube Studio.

    Supports public media from compatible sites such as SoundCloud, Instagram, X, Facebook, TikTok, Vimeo, and Reddit when the extractor can access them. Private or restricted content is unavailable. MP3 and M4A conversion needs FFmpeg. Quality depends on what each site exposes.

    GOOD TO KNOW

    Frequently asked questions.

    Is it free to use?

    Yes. The open-source mode runs in your browser with no app usage quota. Its speed depends on your device, and the model downloads on first use.

    Can I translate a transcript?

    Yes. Translate works with both YouTube captions and uploaded audio. Select the original language and one of 16 translation languages.

    Can I edit or download the transcript?

    Yes. Use Edit text, Remove selected, Copy, or Download .txt in the transcript workspace.

    Does it transcribe videos without captions?

    For a video without captions, choose the open-source mode and upload an audio file you have permission to use. A YouTube link alone cannot provide its audio to the browser model.
